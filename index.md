workshops-heading: Workshops & Training
workshops-description: |-
  Practical workshops for your engineering, finance and sustainability teams. *GreenOps* covers measuring and reducing the environmental footprint of cloud systems. *FinOps* covers how teams work together to get more business value from cloud spend.
  We walk practitioners through processing AWS *Cost and Usage Reports*, querying them with open source tools, and enriching them with environmental impact data using [SPRUCE](https://opensourcegreenops.cloud/), our open source GreenOps platform.
  Workshops are a low commitment way to build capability in house, and often where larger engagements start.
advisory-image: "/images/green-ops.png"
advisory-heading: Advisory
advisory-description: |-
  Measuring the environmental impact of cloud is hard. Provider data is incomplete or misleading, the tooling landscape is fragmented, and the standards are still emerging.
  We start from the context that already exists in your organisation: the FinOps tools you have adopted, the sustainability objectives you are committed to, the teams already involved. From there we help you build a GreenOps practice that fits.
  We advise on best practices, on organisational patterns (the right roles, methodology and profiles to hire), and on both commercial and open source tooling. We are not tied to any vendor, and our recommendations come from work we have done ourselves.
  We are certified *FinOps practitioners* and look at cost and carbon together.
